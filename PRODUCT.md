# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Both technical users familiar with FFmpeg wanting a fast, local workflow, and non-technical users seeking a safe, ad-free alternative to sketchy "convert your file online" services. They have a media file on their device and need it processed quickly without uploading it to a third party.

## Product Purpose

To provide a secure, browser-native media converter that processes files locally. One-click recipes cover the common conversions; for anything unusual, an optional helper builds a prompt for the user's own AI and parses the reply back into a command — keeping the underlying FFmpeg engine visible, inspectable, and editable.

## Brand Personality

Plain, trustworthy, capable.

## Anti-references

This should not look bloated with excessive buttons and options. Avoid the cluttered, ad-heavy aesthetic of free online converters. Advanced settings and debug information must be hidden away from the main surface until explicitly requested.

## Design Principles

- **Progressive disclosure:** Keep the main surface clean and focused on the immediate task. Hide debug logs, advanced settings, and secondary options behind secondary non intrusive views.
- **Focus on the file and intent:** The source media and what the user wants to do with it should be the center of gravity.
- **Local trust:** Visually reinforce that processing happens safely and privately on the user's device.
- **Transparent power:** Keep the actual FFmpeg command visible and editable for those who want to inspect or learn, without overwhelming those who just want the final result.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard web accessibility practices (WCAG AA). No specific exceptional requirements.
